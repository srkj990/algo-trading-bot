📖 Strategy Guide

Pre-session checklist

1. Check Nifty / SGX Nifty futures — gap-up or gap-down?
2. Check India VIX — <14 calm | 14–18 normal | >18 elevated
3. Check global cues — Dow, Nasdaq overnight close
4. Any scheduled event? RBI policy, budget, expiry, major results

Step-by-step decision flow

STEP 1 — VIX > 18? → prefer intraday_options (defined loss) Gap > 0.5%? → intraday_options ATM_ORB or intraday_futures Scheduled event? → intraday_options only, conservative sizing STEP 2 — SELECT ENGINE Intraday, equity only → intraday_equity Intraday, leveraged → intraday_futures Intraday, defined loss → intraday_options Overnight swing, equity → delivery_equity Overnight swing, leveraged → futures_equity Overnight, options play → options_equity STEP 3 — SELECT STRATEGY Trend day → MA, BREAKOUT (or ATM_MOMENTUM for options) Range day → RSI (or skip options) Gap-and-go → ORB or ATM_ORB Post-consolidation → BREAKOUT or ATM_BREAKOUT_EXPANSION Failed breakout → ATM_TRAP_REVERSAL (small size) Uncertain → AUTO_ADAPTIVE STEP 4 — SELECT RISK STYLE First week / uncertain → CONSERVATIVE (0.5%) Normal conditions → BALANCED (1.0%) Strong conviction → AGGRESSIVE (1.5%) STEP 5 — TUNE CONFIG IF NEEDED (see Config Knobs tab) STEP 6 — RUN BACKTEST FIRST (recent 5–10 days)

Intraday Options — ATM Strategy Reference (ranked best → situational)

#StrategyHow It WorksEntry ConditionWorks Best WhenAvoid When
1ATM_MULTI Combines 4 sub-signals (momentum, ORB, VWAP reversion, breakout expansion) — fires when 2+ agree RSI+VWAP+breakout aligned, or ORB + momentum agree Any session type — adapts. Best all-round daily choice. Extreme low-volume sessions (data gaps cause false signals)
2ATM_ORB Measures first 15-candle range. Buys CE when price breaks above high, buys PE when breaks below low Close outside ORB high/low with RSI confirmation Gap-and-go opens (>0.3%), RBI/budget days, strong pre-open cues Flat opens, post-lunch sessions, expiry-day premium crush
3ATM_BREAKOUT_EXPANSION Waits for 45-candle price compression (tight range), then fires on breakout with ATR expansion + volume spike compression < 0.4% range, then close > high/low with ATR expanding Post-consolidation trending moves (e.g. SENSEX stuck 11:00–12:30 then breaks 12:30–13:00) Already-expanded sessions, thin volume (volume check may never pass)
4ATM_TRAP_REVERSAL Detects failed breakout: price pierces key level then reverses hard. Enters in the reversal direction. Prior high/low broken, then engulfing reversal candle with body > avg Key resistance tests that fail, squeeze setups, false breakouts at round numbers Trending markets — counter-trend trades get stopped repeatedly
5ATM_MOMENTUM RSI + VWAP alignment + breakout high/low. Uses staged entry: detects setup candle then waits for pullback before entering Close > VWAP, RSI > 60, close > prior 5-candle high → BUY_CE Strong trending intraday sessions, post-news continuation moves Whipsaw / sideways sessions — staged pullback never comes or whips back
6ATM_IV_EXPANSION Watches IV (implied volatility) for a sudden spike — enters when IV 15m change exceeds threshold, indicating a volatility event igniting IV 15m change > threshold + directional bias from price Event ignition (RBI, FII data, global move), sudden VIX jump Normal sessions — low IV change means no signal ever fires
7ATM_VWAP_REVERSION Fades extended moves back to VWAP — buys when price is stretched far below VWAP, sells when far above Price deviation from VWAP > 0.35%, recent range expanding Sideways sessions with clear VWAP pulls and reversions Trending sessions — price can stay far from VWAP for hours, generating stop-outs

ControlDefaultPurpose
daily_max_loss_pct3%Blocks all entries once daily loss exceeds 3% of capital

Do NOT override consecutive_loss_limit on losing days. It gates you out before daily_max_loss_pct kicks in — let it work.

Common mistakes and fixes

MistakeWhy It HurtsFix
MA on a sideways dayFalse crossovers in range → repeated stop-outsSwitch to RSI or AUTO_ADAPTIVE on flat-VIX days
Buying options on low-VIX daysCheap premium + small moves → theta winsPrefer equity strategies; if options, use tiny premium targets
Increasing size after lossesRevenge trading → accelerated drawdownStep down to CONSERVATIVE after 2 losses
delivery_equity on event daysOvernight gaps can skip past stop lossesAvoid fresh delivery entries 1 day before major events
Ignoring entry_cutoffLate entries have no time to work + face sq-off pressureKeep entry_cutoff ≥30 min before square-off
AUTO_ADAPTIVE without backtestUnknown behaviour in your symbolsRun ≥10 days backtest before live AUTO_ADAPTIVE
Over-allocating to one symbolMax loss on one bad trade is too highKeep max_symbol_allocation at default 20–25%
Ignoring IV percentileHigh IV → IV crush erases gains even if direction rightLower max_buy_iv_percentile on high-VIX days
Skipping paper modeSurprises in order flow, fills, timingAlways run 1–2 days paper when switching engine/strategy
